# Systems-First Senior/Staff Positioning and Transparent React Proof

The portfolio opens with no brand mark or logo—placing **Amr Sadat Abou Hegab** directly as a centered typographic centerpiece—and anchors on **Senior Frontend Engineer** targeting **Senior / Staff Frontend (IC architecture track) roles in Egypt or Remote**. Copy is written in calm, first-person, evidence-led prose whose Hero and About narrative leads with high-stakes national-scale and enterprise architecture (**IDEMIA** national border control & e-Visa systems into **SiliconExpert** global electronics supply-chain platforms, while **Experience** remains reverse-chronological per ADR-0003), positions **AI-Augmented Engineering** (Claude Code, Devin AI, OpenCode) as a disciplined secondary pillar grounded in architectural ownership and verification, and frames the **React + TypeScript** portfolio codebase candidly via a brief colophon and About note as living proof of framework-agnostic craft alongside 7+ years of production Angular (2–22).

## Considered Options

- **Angular-only specialist positioning (or hiding the React implementation)**: Rejected because it filters out strong React-shop Senior/Staff roles that value deep frontend architecture, and hiding the React build wastes a credible proof point of cross-framework adaptability.
- **Equal Angular + React headline billing**: Rejected because claiming equal enterprise tenure in React risks credibility gaps under Staff-level technical probing; honest distinction between 7+ years of production Angular depth and living React + TypeScript craft is stronger.
- **AI-first hero headline ("AI-Native Frontend Engineer")**: Rejected because AI tool headlines age quickly and trigger skepticism among Staff/Principal engineers unless anchored beneath proven systems architecture and verification discipline.
- **Senior / Lead Frontend (Gulf & Regional) positioning**: Rejected in favor of **Senior / Staff Frontend (Egypt or Remote)** to emphasize IC architectural leadership without people-management ambiguity and keep remote scope global rather than region-narrowed.
- **Monogram / brand logo with shortened `Amr Sadat` hero title**: Rejected so the full name **Amr Sadat Abou Hegab** carries the centered typographic identity directly, matching the restrained Daylight gallery mood.

## Consequences

- Hero and navigation layouts must not introduce a brand monogram/logo and must accommodate **Amr Sadat Abou Hegab** cleanly across desktop and mobile viewports (allowing a balanced multi-line display lockup on smaller screens).
- All positioning copy, **Case Studies**, and future artifacts must use **AI-Augmented Engineering** (avoiding *AI-assisted*, *AI-native*, and *vibe coding*). Top-level Hero and About narrative copy leads with IDEMIA into SiliconExpert, while the **Experience** section stays reverse-chronological (**SiliconExpert** first, then **IDEMIA Egypt**) per ADR-0003.
- Public contact surfaces are strictly restricted to **Email** (`amrsadat2017@gmail.com`, tokenized for future custom-domain swap), **LinkedIn**, and **GitHub** (`github.com/amr-sadat`) alongside `Egypt · UTC+2`; personal phone/WhatsApp, street address, military status, DOB, and inactive social accounts must never be published on the site.
